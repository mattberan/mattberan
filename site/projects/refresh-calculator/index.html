What does keeping laptops longer really cost?

Most refresh cycles are set by the depreciation schedule. This calculator adds the costs that schedule leaves out, so you can walk into the budget conversation with your own numbers.

Download Excel model

Costs shown are per device per year, averaged across the full cycle. Resale value is assumed to drop by about half for each year past year 3.

The default values are illustrative starting points, not benchmarks. Swap in your own ticket, repair and payroll data for numbers you can defend.

Made by Matt Beran, host of the Ticket Volume podcast.

This is the one number to keep

The calculator will not be accurate without the loaded cost of one employee hour. It drives downtime and the time lost to slow devices, the biggest costs of older laptops.

If you don't know it, use the estimate of an hour. It's a low number for most organizations. Without it, every result will lean toward keeping laptops forever.
