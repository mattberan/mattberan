The Take

Everything has changed twice in the last 10 years... are you ready for it to happen again?

On an OpenServiceCommunity call this week, the topic was about independent consulting and contracting.

One challenging part is quitting your source of money... leaving a paycheck.

And one of the presenters pointed out that you really can't do REALLY QUITE unless you have 3-6 months of pay because "do you remember the pandemic and how everything changed quickly?"

And it made me think about how we are likely ALL adapting to needing to adapt; our trust in a consistent, calm lifestyle has been degraded.

Whether we realize it or not; our collective appetite for risk has gone down significantly. Makes me wish I had taken Macroeconomics.

AI/Job Shift/Economy - inflation... lack of trust in leadership...

I would love to hear from you - has your riskiness dropped down? Are you less likely to try something risky like making a big purchase or changing careers?

Everything you need to know. Nothing you don't.
