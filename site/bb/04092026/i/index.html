The Take

I got busted using AI this week.

I'll admit it - I did it. I handed in my homework without reading it.

Was the evaluation committee also VERY kind to me? YES.

Did I fully admit to what I did? Also YES.

Thankfully, I've got editors for my stuff - because I was phoning it in last week.

I fell into the very trap I tell others about. And now, I move back to discipline. Re-working the sloppy slop and focusing again on quality instead of speed.

So it's greasy - a slippery slope. AND - I value that friction greater than ever. Having an editor, a team to tell you when your stuff doesn't hit. A lot of AI leaders talking about "Human in the Loop" or HITL - let's just give some attention to the team who got yo back TTWGYB

So that's one of my guardrails.

And that's what we need to make visible to the technical leaders within our organizations.

Help them understand their slippery slope.

•Where will you trust AI too much, and how can you build a safety mechanism into the system itself?
•Where will your team be tempted to solve problems that introduce harm or risk beyond what is acceptable to your teams, people and profits?
•What data do you need to sequester to protect?
•What data do you need to organize to leverage?

What other questions are you asking your colleagues as it pertains to advanced automation (AA)?

Post them in the comments, respond to my email - let's start a discussion on finding guardrails
